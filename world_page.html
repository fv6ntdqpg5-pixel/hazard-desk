            Sources. Tropical storms, floods and volcano alerts: GDACS, the Global Disaster Alert and Coordination System of the United Nations and the European Commission, with storm tracks from the national and joint warning centers. Erupting volcanoes: the Smithsonian Institution's Global Volcanism Program. U.S. volcano alert levels: USGS. Earthquakes: USGS, magnitude 4.5 and up. Tsunami messages: NOAA's tsunami warning centers. Sea temperature: NASA GIBS, MUR daily analysis from JPL.

            Not an official warning service. This page summarizes public data and only helps while it is open. A tsunami message from NOAA's centers is advice to national authorities outside the United States; each country decides its own warnings. Storm wind zones are GDACS estimates, not official forecast cones.
