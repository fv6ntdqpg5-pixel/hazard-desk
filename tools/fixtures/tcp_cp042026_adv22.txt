DISCUSSION AND OUTLOOK
----------------------
At 500 PM HST (0300 UTC), the center of Tropical Storm Pena was 
located near latitude 9.8 North, longitude 170.6 West. Pena is 
moving toward the west near 15 mph (24 km/h), and this general 
motion is expected for the next few days.
 
Maximum sustained winds are near 45 mph (75 km/h) with higher gusts. 
Pena is forecast to begin weakening early Thursday and dissipate by 
late Friday or early Saturday. 

Tropical-storm-force winds extend outward up to 115 miles (185 km)
from the center.
 
The estimated minimum central pressure is 1002 mb (29.59 inches).
